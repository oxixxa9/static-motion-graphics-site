Spatial Layout Rules

  • Outer Padding: padding: 56px 64px; Ensures safe zone on all devices.
  • Headline to Card Gap: Fixed 36px - 44px spacing between bottom of subtitle and top of primary card.
  • Z-Index Layering:
    • Layer 0: Mesh Canvas Gradient
    • Layer 1: Topographic SVG contour lines (opacity 0.12)
    • Layer 2: Cloud silhouettes (top-left & bottom-right)
    • Layer 3: Paper UI cards & widgets
    • Layer 4: Floating cursor pointers & unread notification badges
